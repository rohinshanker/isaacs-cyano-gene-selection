**GSE205443-01 — Unsupported continuous-light classification.** Class: **disallowed fill**. Locations: `data/expression/ingest/GSE205443.json:56` and all nine layer overrides; `data/expression/sources.json:2330` and the other eight records; `site/data/meta.json:1`; renderer `site/js/ui/data-sources.js:139`. Exact shipped assertions: `"status": "reported"`, `"kind": "continuous"`, and “low fluorescent light, continuous (photoperiod not stated)”. The list renders “continuous” without that parenthesis. P, Methods, “RB-TnSeq Experiment” (`sec7`) specifies intensity and fluorescent lighting, but does not report a photoperiod or continuous illumination. No continuous-light statement occurs elsewhere in the retrieved article; the nine GEO sample protocols do not supply it. **Effect: medium** — readers can filter or group these assays as verified continuous-light experiments. **Reproduce:** inspect each GSE205443 `record.conditions.lightRegime`, then open Fitness screen and its Light regime cells/source details; compare with P `sec7`. **Confidence: high** that “reported continuous” exceeds the located evidence; this does not establish that the cultures were actually under a light/dark cycle.

**GSE205443-04 — Unit definition conflates gene fitness with a simple barcode-abundance ratio.** Class: **unit or transformation mismatch**. Locations: `data/expression/ingest/GSE205443.json:32`; `data/expression/sources.json:2385` and the other eight records; `site/data/meta.json:1`; `site/data/citations.json:95`. Exact unit: “gene fitness (log2 ratio of barcode abundance in the fraction to Time 0, Wetmore et al. 2015), as published in Supplementary File S4”. P “RB-TnSeq Data Analysis” (`sec8`) invokes W’s calculation. W, Methods `s3.15`–`s3.18`, distinguishes strain log2 ratios from gene fitness: the latter combines insertion-strain fitness with weights, pseudocount handling, and scaffold/location normalization. **Effect: medium** — a reader may try to reconstruct the value as one raw gene/barcode count ratio or treat zero as an unnormalized no-change ratio. This is a definition defect, not a faulty ingestion transform. **Reproduce:** compare the shipped unit with W “BarSeq data analysis and calculation of gene fitness”, “Gene fitness”, and “Normalization”; S4’s Fitness columns are the values actually ingested. **Confidence: high**.
