# Biological decisions for lab review

Use this list when discussing the UTEX 2973 site with collaborators, and revisit
the relevant row for any exported panel proposed for an experiment. These are
scientific interpretation and future evidence-admission decisions. Publishing
the current, clearly labelled site does not approve a particular panel or require
the lab to settle every row. Record a reviewer, date, decision, and supporting
evidence in the experiment record before acting on a changed assumption.

| Priority | Decision to review with collaborators | Current site decision and evidence boundary | Useful reviewer / source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | For an actual 6–10-gene panel, are the selected loci, gene models, recoding map, and constraints appropriate for the intended experiment? | The export reproduces a computational design, not a biological approval. Check the pinned UTEX 2973 locus identities, starts, overlaps, discontinuous CDSs, stop handling, and `prfB` frameshift against planned constructs. | Experimental lead and genome-annotation reviewer; [panel checklist](AAA-manual-review-checklist.md#2-review-the-actual-biological-panel), [genome provenance](genome-provenance.md). |
| 2 | Should any of the ten audited CAI-reference candidates enter a revised reference set, and what independent evidence defines a suitable reference? | The published 71 loci remain fixed. Product-label agreement and the TypeSafe audit do not establish high expression; changing membership also changes CAI weights, proxy ranks, and downstream panels. | Translation/physiology reviewer; [CAI audit and exact loci](cai-reference-set.md). |
| 3 | Is genomic tRNA copy-number adaptiveness a useful proxy for this panel, and what evidence would justify a later expression or charging tier? | The 44 RefSeq tRNAs agree with a tRNAscan-SE 2.0 rerun; one extra low-confidence pseudo candidate is excluded. This supports computational plausibility, not tRNA abundance, charging, or decoding. The published tAI stays unchanged; future disputed calls receive sensitivity scenarios and targeted structural review before any change. | tRNA/translation specialist; [tRNA validation](trna-annotation-validation.md), [metric conventions](metric-convention-parity.md). |
| 4 | What evidence would justify labelling a UTEX 2973 protein as directly detected? | Every plotted CDS has an exact RefSeq protein sequence, but the inspected PASS00399 deposit has no reproducible accepted per-locus protein list under the study's MSGF+ criteria. | 5 | Which functional claims are safe to use for priority genes, especially when based on homologs or computational GO? | Tested UTEX `atpA`, `ppnK`, and `rpaA` alleles are shown first with their assay conditions. The approved category layer has 13 exact reviewed rows; a separate derived layer (row 11) colours further CDSs only under `pcc-7942-derived` or `go-iea-derived` labels. | Gene-function specialist; [protein evidence](protein-evidence.md), [function-category contract](function-categories.md), [annotation evidence](annotation-release-readiness.md#go-annotation-contract). |
| 6 | What native gene-body expression evidence would be adequate to add an abundance axis? | Tan 2018 supplies UTEX 2973 start-site initiation counts, not whole-gene abundance. No replicated genome-wide native abundance matrix is admitted; narrower or nonreplicated published measurements are not a default quantitative substitute. | Cyanobacterial transcriptomics and statistics reviewers; [future-source admission gate](future-data-roadmap.md), [Tan measurement boundary](../../data/expression/TAN2018_TSS_PROVENANCE.md#what-it-measures-and-what-it-does-not). |
| 7 | Should PCC 7942 evidence influence any panel choice, and under which cross-strain caveats? | Borrowed PCC 7942 abundance is opt-in and explicitly cross-strain. Adomako 2022 redistributes Rubin 2015 PCC 7942 essentiality calls; the site shows them only at exact, unambiguous shared-protein joins. Treat applying each call to a UTEX 2973 candidate as an assumption, not a UTEX measurement. [Ungerer et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1128/mBio.02327-17) reports similar growth at PCC-compatible light but different optimal growth, and Rubin's screen used other conditions. Missing or ambiguous calls never mean nonessential. | Comparative-genomics and experimental reviewers; [PCC essentiality policy](pcc-essentiality.md), [expression policy](guided-panel-design.md), [crosswalk](annotation-release-readiness.md#identifier-crosswalk-contract). |
| 8 | Can Tan 2018 start sites support a proposed regulatory interpretation or construct boundary? | Gene-linked gTSS and separate non-gTSS features are searchable with each source condition. TSS counts measure initiation, not gene-body abundance. Historical/current start differences, a published high-light duration discrepancy, potential antisense targets, and a pinned Table S1/S8 value conflict require locus-specific review; coordinate proximity alone does not prove regulation. | Transcriptomics/regulatory reviewer; [Tan provenance](../../data/expression/TAN2018_TSS_PROVENANCE.md). |
| 9 | Does a short gene's native PCA position reflect meaningful codon use for the proposed experiment? | The PCA remains fixed when filters change. Short CDSs have more zero RSCU values and PC2 has measured length/sparsity sensitivity; downsampling explains part, not all, of the shift. Use the direct metric plot and inspect length before interpreting an outlier. The below-75-nt flag is a fixed annotation review trigger; none of the current CDSs fall below it. | Quantitative-genomics reviewer; [PCA audit](pca-length-sensitivity.md), [length cohorts](length-cohorts.md). |
| 10 | Are the rare-codon and local-folding conventions useful for the planned perturbation? | Rare means within-synonym frequency below 0.1 in this genome, not measured slow translation. On-demand MFE and delta-MFE are model outputs, not expression, fitness, or viability evidence. Assess start-window context and any proposed change with gene-specific experiments. | Translation and RNA-structure reviewers; [metric conventions](current-design-answers.md#what-do-rare-codon-cai-and-tai-mean-here), [folding contract](rna-folding.md). |
| 11 | **Accept or reject the source-derived category layer, its rubric, and its 0.8 threshold.** | Built 2026-09-22, awaiting your decision. TypeSafe `jev-1.13.0` judged the eleven-label category from the joined PCC 7942 product name (2,542 loci; 1,093 assigned) and from GO IEA terms (1,584 loci; 1,028 assigned); both agree on 745 of the 758 loci where both assign and disagree on 13. With every source on the map colours 13 CDSs by review, 1,352 by a derived source (332 PCC only, 268 GO only, 752 both), 13 as disagreeing, and 1,350 unknown; per category under all sources: photosynthesis 88, carbon and nutrient 282, ATP and respiration 56, pigment and cofactor 133, translation 168, DNA and RNA 122, transport and envelope 276, signalling and circadian 118, stress and repair 56, other characterised 52. Evaluation: 62 of 63 predeclared labels exact, zero wrong categories assigned; blinded spot check by the agent, not a biologist: GO 26 of 28, PCC 24 of 34, with three real PCC errors on amino-acid-named cofactor enzymes. The 13 reviewed rows are unchanged and always win; derived colour is hollow-marked and labelled, and the UTEX 2973-only toggle restores the reviewed-only view. Rejecting means deleting the derived file, which the site tolerates. | Gene-function specialist; [derived-category evidence](source-derived-categories.md), [function-category contract](function-categories.md). |
