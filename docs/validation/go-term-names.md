# GO term-name lookup

`site/data/go-term-names-v1.json` is a deterministic, compact lookup for the
exact 1,120 GO identifiers in the pinned UTEX 2973 annotation release. Each
entry preserves the May 19 ontology's primary `name`, `namespace`, and
`isObsolete` status. It does not infer, strengthen, or reinterpret gene
function, and it never follows `replaced_by` or `consider` relationships.

## Independent source dates

The gene annotation source and term-name ontology are pinned separately:

- `data/annotation/source/GCF_000817325.1_ASM81732v1_gene_ontology.gaf.gz`
  was generated by NCBI on 2026-05-14 against GO version 2026-03-25.
- Term labels come from the official GO Consortium `go-basic.obo` archive dated
  **2026-05-19**. This later ontology date is lookup provenance only and does
  not change evidence, qualifiers, aspects, or assignment dates in the GAF.

Ten exact GAF IDs are obsolete in the May 19 ontology and occur in 18 annotation
rows. They remain keyed and searchable with their source labels and
`isObsolete: true`; consumers must not treat them as current category evidence.
No replacement is inferred, because several IDs have multiple `consider`
targets or no replacement.

The JSON records the ontology URL, release date, byte size and SHA-256, plus
the SHA-256 checksums of the derived annotation TSV and source GAF. The 32 MB
OBO archive is deliberately not committed.

## Rebuild and check

Acquire the pinned ontology outside the repository, then rebuild and verify:

```sh
curl -o /tmp/go-basic-2026-05-19.obo \
  https://release.geneontology.org/2026-05-19/ontology/go-basic.obo
python3 tools/go_term_names.py build --obo /tmp/go-basic-2026-05-19.obo
python3 tools/go_term_names.py check --obo /tmp/go-basic-2026-05-19.obo
python3 -m unittest tests.test_go_term_names
```

The builder requires exactly 32,212,518 bytes with SHA-256
`923645000afcb1df0e72e252b64950b1c2bcc98c9393fec8c1dd1a0f7c9d4d77`
and `data-version: releases/2026-05-19`. It also rejects changed GAF or
annotation TSV inputs, missing or duplicate IDs, invalid namespaces, or any
change to the expected 10 obsolete IDs and 18 affected annotation rows.
